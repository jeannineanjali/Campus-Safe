Resources

Emergency procedures and campus contacts.

Fire
  1. Raise the alarm and call the fire officer.
  2. Leave by the nearest exit. Do not use lifts.
  3. Gather at the assembly point and wait for a head count.
Medical emergency
  1. Call campus medical or 112.
  2. Do not move the person unless in danger.
  3. Send someone to meet the ambulance at the gate.
Threat or lockdown
  1. Move to a secure room and lock or block the door.
  2. Silence your phone and stay out of sight.
  3. Call security when it is safe to do so.
